Forecast assumptions and the meaning of exhaustion

The 2026 datasets have different dates and denominators: tracker generated 24 November (39 institutions / 260 threads / 138 statutory requests; mailbox through 18 October), request-status register generated 24 November (261 records / 53 lanes; primarily as of 21 October), 6 November snapshot (318 conversations), and 21 November roadmap (84 lanes / 924 steps). They are retained separately; do not add them together.

There are 15 Tier 1 and 35 Tier 2 first-contact slots. Each is conditional on reconciling the whole 2026 file and checking no duplicate or live matter. Tier 3's 15 lanes are review-only and open on trigger; Tier 4's 23 lanes are no-contact relevance checks. Calendar inclusion does not make every organisation an FOI body.

Response, chaser, internal-review and remedy items are decision checkpoints, not automatic sends or universally valid deadlines. Recalculate actual clocks against the chosen regime, receipt and institution's current procedure. Private bodies get voluntary records enquiries; SAR is only for personal data.

A lane is exhausted only when relevant free avenues have been used, ruled out with reason, found not applicable, refused or unavailable. Paid copies, subscriptions, licences, fees and court/tribunal routes stop at PAUSE—NO SPEND. Regulator and ombudsman decisions may carry into 2028; G4 records the true disposition rather than forcing closure.

The weekly review records time used per useful evidence result and checks the outbound cap. A chaser or complaint displaces a planned first contact if needed to remain under two messages on that day.

Calendar is planning metadata only. No external request has been drafted or sent. Source baseline: 26 November 2026.
