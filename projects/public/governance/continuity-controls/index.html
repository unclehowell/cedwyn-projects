Continuity and Research Controls

A source-led research and reconstruction programme designed to continue safely when the Executive is not supervising every routine step.

Current stop state · 11 November 2026: the case-reopening gate remains on standby. No trigger event is accepted as established; no finding of fraud or concealment is made; no external allegation or report is authorised.

The main research flywheel

Evidence in, verified outputs out
  1. Discover records, maps, registers, catalogues, planning documents and institutions across the full Llandough–Leckwith landscape.
  2. Qualify the custodian, date, parcel identity, legal access route and whether the enquiry is genuinely new.
  3. Prepare a focused FOIA, EIR, statutory HER-access or archive enquiry; track each authority's own clock and procedure.
  4. Preserve original replies and attachments with source metadata and provenance.
  5. Catalogue records and relationships; distinguish what each source establishes from what remains uncertain.
  6. Challenge chronology and parcel reconstruction against contrary records and alternative explanations.
  7. Publish only sourced, privacy-checked and independently reviewed material.
  8. Feed each verified answer into the next precise missing-record or custodian search.

A request is not a result. An acknowledgement is not disclosure. A catalogue entry is not inspection of the original record.

The nested threshold-watch loop

Private candidate review only

This loop records potentially material discrepancies without labelling them misconduct. Each candidate is tested for source authenticity, person/property identity, independent corroboration, contradictory evidence, innocent alternatives, materiality and the exact legal test.

  1. Log a neutral observation and its original source.
  2. Verify the document, date, issuer, parcel and provenance.
  3. Seek independent corroboration and record contradictions.
  4. Test alternative explanations and identify missing evidence.
  5. Map each required legal limb to the evidence and what remains unproved.
  6. Obtain independent challenge before an Executive decision.

Candidate is not a finding. The loop authorises no external report. Reopening remains on standby unless every legal limb is independently supported and the Executive expressly authorises a next step.

Fail-safe and continuity controls

Checkpoint · freshness · stop
  • Every extract records when it was generated, source scope and whether all pages and distinct IDs were checked.
  • Old snapshots remain dated historical snapshots; mismatched query totals are not used to calculate current completion percentages.
  • Each scheduled run records its checkpoint, source timestamp, completed work, failed integrations and next action.
  • When a connector, pagination, integrity check or deployment verification fails, the last known-good public output is preserved and dependent publication is paused.
  • Changes are staged on a branch, tested and proposed in a pull request. A PR is not a production deployment; live delivery is verified separately.

Human approval boundaries

Autonomy without uncontrolled escalation

Routine research, record ingestion, deduplication, evidence tagging, private draft preparation and technical checks may proceed within the approved mandate. The Executive retains approval for external sending, legal filings, regulatory reports, payments, public disputed claims, land acquisition, irreversible account/repository changes and production releases.

Recipient-facing drafts are kept in the private HubSpot review lane and must clear the applicable Hive review gate. Only the Executive sends. Public outputs pass provenance, privacy, rights, accuracy, accessibility and build checks.

UK policy and records framework

Use the right procedure for each record
  • Freedom of Information Act 2000 and the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.
  • Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 and Welsh Historic Environment Record guidance, where relevant.
  • The section 46 Code of Practice on Records Management and the relevant holder's retention/disposal rules.
  • PeopleCert: PRINCE2 Project Management (Version 7). This programme uses a tailored internal approach and does not claim PeopleCert certification.

This is an operating framework, not legal advice. Each authority, requested information set and procedure must be assessed separately.

The detailed operating protocol is maintained in the Project Office source repository. Public pages contain no private candidate reviews, personal correspondence, private contact information or legal advice.
